A Little About Me

Rooted in curiosity and classical discipline, my creative path began with pencil sketches capturing subtle nuances of human expression. Over the years, that exploration blossomed into textured mixed media and evocative oil landscapes that honor warmth, quietness, and atmosphere.

For me, the studio is not simply a workshop—it is an intimate laboratory of observation. Each canvas is allowed to breathe through thin, luminous veils of pigment, delicate graphite strokes, and deliberate marks of texture that invite slow contemplation.

Whether portraying timeless cultural icons or still-life moments caught in fleeting afternoon light, my goal is always to reveal the quiet divinity and profound emotion within everyday perception.
